# P24 — "Jacaranda in print": the seductive landing, a living Home, Explore, share cards (the owner's brief, 2026-10-07)

The owner: "I want a world class UI and user experience that is seductive … copy the design skills there [the three
references] … add more relevant features like a real full-stack engineer … make it dynamic rather than static, some
sections moving and showing life … cute images from the internet where relevant … /impeccable for polishing,
/frontend-design for designing, Playwright for responsiveness … do not stop until the design is up to the standards."
Direction: D-66. Authority order: CLAUDE.md, docs/spec/07, spec 04 §4.6 (simple UI), D-66, this card, then the skills.

## Acceptance (what "up to the standards" means here, measurable)

1. The ux-reviewer and the reviewer PASS; the owner's own look at the screenshot set is the final word.
2. Lighthouse (mobile default, Slow 4G) on `/`, `/explore`, `/dev`: performance ≥ 90 and accessibility ≥ 90, light and
   dark; LCP ≤ 2.5 s on `/` with the photographs in (D-53's band is not a licence to regress).
3. Every route ≤ 150,000 B gzipped JS; axe strict 0 at 360 and 1440, light and dark; one `[data-primary]` per screen;
   ≤ 2 chips per card; 44 px targets; no sideways scroll at 360; reduced motion respected everywhere something moves.
4. No fabricated figure, testimonial, logo or claim: stats are product constants or seeded figures labelled "Seeded
   example"; photographs are licensed and credited; copy changes tagged `[[COPY-REVIEW]]`/`[[SW-REVIEW]]` with en/sw parity.
5. The full Playwright suite, the clock scenarios and the demo story stay green; CI as the standing exceptions.

## Build (three worktrees, ≤ 3 implementers)

- **A. Photographs** (researcher → `frontend/public/photos/`): eight to ten Kenyan photographs from Wikimedia Commons
  (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA only): Nairobi (skyline, a jacaranda avenue), Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, a market, a
  tower site or a workshop; vendored as AVIF and WebP at 1600 and 800 px with a 24 px blur placeholder, under 120 KB
  each; `frontend/public/photos/CREDITS.md` and `docs/platform/research/photos.md` with title, author, licence, source
  URL and the exact attribution line; a `/credits` page lists them (the footer links it).
- **B. Backend** (impl-backend): `GET /api/public/activity` (the last 20 anonymised, seeded-or-real events: a problem
  posted, a version registered, an engagement reaching a stage, a Brief opened; no names, no titles beyond the public
  teaser title, cached 60 s, rate-limited, under the existing public-read rules) and `GET /api/public/explore`
  (published problems grouped by county and by niche with counts and the newest three teasers each; public data only);
  seeds unchanged; tests; openapi regenerated.
  Deferred: stage_reached needs an app_public_stage_events() definer function from db-migrations (next revision).
- **C. Frontend** (impl-frontend, the design skills): the Fraunces display face (subset, swap), the paper canvas and
  the P20 addendum; the landing rebuilt in the D-66 order (nav; the hero with the italic accent, the live product panel
  with the ticking countdown and the "Demo data" chip, the two actions; a stats row of product constants; the county
  photo strip with a slow pan and the county names, linking to Explore; How it works as three numbered cards with mono
  chips; the dark "every recommendation names its reason" band with a terminal-style why-panel from the ranker's real
  reason codes; What you can do; the "What's happening" ticker over `/api/public/activity`, paused on hover, static
  under reduced motion, labelled when seeded; Proof of authorship; Questions; the closing band; the richer footer with
  Credits); `/explore` (public: county tiles with the photographs and counts, niche rows, the newest teasers, one primary
  "Create an account"); the signed-in Home with life (the time-of-day greeting, the countdown, a soft pulse on the "Your
  turn" mark, the "What's happening" strip under the stat tiles, seeded-labelled); `app/manifest.ts` and icons (an
  installable PWA for the Android use scene; no service worker yet); Open Graph share images for the public teaser and
  the verify page through `next/og` (`/og/idea/[id]`, `/og/verify/[id]`; no new dependency); `_meta.reviewP24`.
- **D. Gate**: the full suite, Lighthouse, budgets, axe, screenshots at 375 and 1440 light and dark into
  `docs/demo/screenshots/p24/`, the walkthrough and README set re-recorded, the ux-reviewer and reviewer, CI, the report.

## Iteration

Bounded passes, not an open loop: build → batched review (reviewer, ux-reviewer, the orchestrator's own look at the
shots) → one fix round → confirmation → gate. A second design pass only if the confirmation still finds a MAJOR or the
owner asks for a change after seeing the screenshots.
