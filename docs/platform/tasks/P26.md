## Why the clock was not seen

P23-3 wired the ticking days/hours/minutes figure (`components/ui/Countdown.tsx` via `TimeLeft`) to Home's "Next
deadline" tile and "Needs you" hero, the tracker's secondary line and a Brief's deadline. The engagement cards of
both lists, Home's Needs-you cards and the organisation's deadline rows print only the business-days sentence
("7 business days left (16 Oct 2026)"), so a person reading their active projects never sees a clock. The owner may
also have been on the pre-P23-3 images (their rebuild was in progress); the fix below makes the clock unmissable
either way.

## Acceptance (measurable)

1. **A — the clock.** Every active engagement with a `due_at` shows, on both portals, a live days · hours · minutes
   figure (`<time dateTime="P…">`, figure face, ticking on the minute from `X-App-Now`): the engagement cards of
   `/dev/engagements` and `/org/engagements`, Home's Needs-you cards (both sides), the organisation's deadline rows,
   and the tracker's figure panel (the business-days words stay as the clock's line). Overdue, on hold, the
   organisation's answer-by case and ended engagements keep their present words; no negative time. Warm tone only
   under 24 h on the viewer's step. No live region. Unit tests for each case and a tick; e2e asserts the figure on a
   developer's and an organisation's card.
2. **B — Companies.** `/dev/companies` lists organisations as cards under their niche headings: place photograph band
   (county first, niche next, lattice last), monogram, the name in Fraunces, the meta line, the verification badge
   verbatim as the one chip, the response record when sent; `/dev/companies/{id}` opens with a hero band, the
   monogram and the name, the badge, an E0 explanation from the existing held-tag sentence, fact tiles, niche chips
   linking to Discover, and one primary action (Discover in the first niche). No logo, no `<img>` with a non-empty
   alt, ≤ 2 chips per card, one `[data-primary]` per screen, no new client JS, Save-Data → no photographs.
3. reviewer PASS and ux-reviewer PASS; vitest, eslint, tsc, prettier, copy-lint green; the full Playwright suite,
   the clock scenarios and the demo story green on the compose stack; every route ≤ 150,000 B gzipped JS; axe
   strict 0 at 360 and 1440 on the engagements lists, a tracker, the companies list and a company page, light and
   dark; Lighthouse (mobile, Slow 4G) performance ≥ 90 and accessibility ≥ 95 on `/dev/engagements`, `/org/engagements`,
   `/dev/companies` and a company page; CI as the standing exceptions (D-42 CodeQL, D-56 npm audit, the backend
   job's limit).
