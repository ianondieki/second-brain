## Acceptance (measurable)

1. reviewer PASS on every worktree, security-reviewer PASS on the backend (the two new signed-in reads), ux-reviewer
   PASS on the frontend; the owner's own look at the screenshot set is the final word.
2. Lighthouse (mobile default, Slow 4G), light and dark, medians of three: performance ≥ 90 and accessibility ≥ 95 on
   `/dev`, `/dev/discover`, `/dev/ideas`, a developer tracker, `/org`, `/org/inbox`, `/admin/moderation`, `/login`;
   LCP ≤ 2.5 s on `/dev` and `/dev/discover`.
3. Every route ≤ 150,000 B gzipped JS (the palette loads on first use; today `/dev/discover` is at 148,176 B and
   `/login` at 148,514 B, so anything added there is paid for by a trim); axe strict 0 on every screen in the
   screenshot set at 360 and 1440, light and dark, motion on and reduced; forced-colors spot check on Home, Discover,
   the tracker and the palette; no sideways scroll at 360.
4. Honesty: photographs licensed and credited, decorative beside a visible niche or county name; no logo; the activity
   calendar shows the person's own counts only; seeded content keeps its labels.
5. The full Playwright suite, the clock scenarios and the demo story stay green; vitest, lint, typecheck, copy-lint,
   api:check; backend ruff, format, mypy --strict, unit and the new integration tests; CI as the standing exceptions.

## Build (≤ 3 implementers at a time, in worktrees)

- **A. Photographs** (researcher, `wt/P25-A`): one licensed Wikimedia Commons photograph per top-level niche without a
  fitting one already (financial services, ICT and telecommunications, education, public sector, health, energy,
  social/NGO; agriculture, logistics and retail may reuse P24's tea, ferry and market photographs or get better ones),
  plus a Nairobi morning photograph for Home's greeting; same formats, sizes, credits and index as P24 (the index
  gains `niche`).
- **Research note** (researcher, scratchpad → `docs/platform/research/ui-principles.md`): the published UI and front-end
  guidance of Addy Osmani, Lea Verou, Sara Soueidan, Dan Abramov, Evan You, Kent C. Dodds, Harry Roberts, Rachel
  Andrew and Paul Irish (and Ryan Dahl on secure defaults), each claim cited, ending in a checklist the implementers apply.
- **B. Backend** (impl-backend, `wt/P25-B`): `GET /api/me/search?q=` (the command palette: the caller's ideas, the
  caller's engagements, readable public problems, listed companies; at most five each; min two characters; rate
  limited) and `GET /api/me/activity?weeks=26` (per-day counts of the caller's own actions by kind, for the calendar);
  both read as the signed-in person under RLS; openapi regenerated; unit and integration tests including leakage.
- **C1. Frontend foundation and the developer portal** (impl-frontend, `wt/P25-C`): the shell (nav rail and tab bar
  refined, the top bar's Search button), `PageHero`, the niche photograph component, the motion primitives (View
  Transitions where supported, press feedback, count-up, skeletons), the HCI floor (forced-colors, prefers-contrast,
  focus ring, container queries, subgrid, `content-visibility`), the command palette (lazy), the activity calendar;
  then Home, Discover (and niches, trends, week), My ideas, the editor, the idea page and certificate, pitch, the
  shared tracker and messages, engagements, companies, peers, teams, quiz, events.
- **C2. Frontend: the organisation portal, the staff console and the rest** (impl-frontend, `wt/P25-D`, started when
  C1's foundation is merged): org Home, Inbox, matches, scouts, shortlist and compare, the full proposal, Problems
  and Briefs, Events, engagements lists; notifications, settings, billing; the staff console; sign-in, sign-up,
  two-step, check-email, verify, help, legal.
- **D. Gate**: the suite, Lighthouse, budgets, axe, the screenshot set `docs/demo/screenshots/p25/` (Home, Discover,
  My ideas, the idea page, the tracker, the palette open, org Home, Inbox, the full proposal, moderation, login; 375
  and 1440, light and dark), the README set and walkthrough re-recorded, reviews, CI, the report, the merge.
