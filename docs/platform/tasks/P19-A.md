# P19-A · Tracker side states: INFO_REQUESTED, EXPIRED, ON_HOLD (REQ-ENG-10 part; AC-PROP-3; AC-TRACK-4 part)

Agent: impl-backend (xhigh; `engagements/` is security-sensitive). Worktree. Depends on P19-M (`engagement_notes`).
Map: scratchpad `p19/map-tracker.md`. Spec: `docs/spec/06` §6.9 side branches, `policy.yaml` `expire_bd`.

## Scope

The DB already knows the states (enums, CHECKs, the chain trigger's "return to the state you left" rule, system
events with `actor_role='system'`). The transition table, commands, deadlines, the expiry job and notifications do not.

1. **`INFO_REQUESTED`.** `Command.REQUEST_INFO` by ORG `DECIDERS` from `SUBMITTED` and `UNDER_REVIEW`, with a body
   `{lock_version, question: str (1–2000)}`; target `INFO_REQUESTED`; the stage clock pauses: the event's
   `stage_deadline_at` is null. `Command.ANSWER_INFO` by the developer from `INFO_REQUESTED`, body `{lock_version,
   answer}`; returns to the state it was entered from (the chain trigger enforces it) with `stage_deadline_at` =
   the deadline that state had + the paused business days (compute from the entering event's `stage_deadline_at`
   and the pause length in Kenyan business days; `calendar.add_business_days`). Both write an `engagement_notes` row
   (`info_request`, `info_answer`) with the event's `seq`. Notices: N03 to the developer on the request, N01-style to
   the organisation on the answer (see 4). `whose_turn`: the developer while paused. `pending()`: one item for the
   developer ("Answer the organisation's question"). `available()`: the organisation cannot decline or approve while
   paused (409 `illegal_transition`), it waits for the answer.
2. **`ON_HOLD` before the agreement.** `Command.PAUSE` by either party (ORG `DECIDERS`, the developer) from any
   `BEFORE_AGREEMENT` state that is not `SUBMITTED` nor a side state, body `{lock_version, reason: 1–500 chars,
   resume_at: date}` with `resume_at` after today and at most `policy.on_hold.max_days` (60) later; target `ON_HOLD`;
   `stage_deadline_at` = end of `resume_at` (so `due` shows the resume date). `Command.RESUME` by either party, or
   by the system at `resume_at`; returns to the entered-from state with its deadline shifted by the hold's length in
   business days. Notes `hold` and `resume`. After `AGREEMENT_SIGNING` the command is refused (409): the
   acknowledgement-or-dispute branch is the next phase (record on the card, not in the API as a half feature).
3. **`EXPIRED` by the system.** A periodic job `jobs/expiry.py` (`@app.periodic` every 15 minutes, like reminders;
   register in `jobs/app.py` IMPORT_PATHS) that, on the shared clock, finds engagements past `expire_bd` of their
   state and appends a system event (`actor_role='system'`, no user, the job bound to the acting party as
   `_ACTING_PARTY` does): `SUBMITTED` → `EXPIRED (NO_REVIEW)` at 20 BD, `UNDER_REVIEW` → `NO_DECISION` at 30 BD,
   `INTEREST_CONFIRMED` → `CONTACT_NOT_MADE` at 10 BD, `ORG_INTEREST` → `NO_DEV_RESPONSE` at 5 BD; the same job
   resumes `ON_HOLD` engagements whose `resume_at` has passed. The tag's `status` becomes `expired` (projection,
   AC-PROP-3). `expire_bd` counts from `stage_entered_at` and is paused by `INFO_REQUESTED` (the shifted deadline
   carries it: expire at `stage_deadline_at + (expire_bd − due_bd)` business days, computed once per engagement).
   Idempotent, one engagement per transaction with the FOR UPDATE lock, never a terminal state twice.
4. **Notifications.** `notify.compose()` returns None for system events today; make expiry and auto-resume notify
   both parties in-app (kinds `engagement.n01`, `n03`, `n05`, `n17` as the matrix rows say; `DECLINE_LABELS`-style
   sentences with the reason in words) and by email through a small branded template (`simple.html.j2`, one
   sentence, one link to the tracker; EM6's full form stays REQ-NOT-05). Requests, answers, pauses and resumes
   notify the other party in-app and by email the same way (N03, N20).
5. **Reminders.** `reminders/health.py` and the digests treat `INFO_REQUESTED` and `ON_HOLD` as paused: no "overdue",
   the render already has the labels (render.py 54-58, 185).
6. **Policy.** `policy.yaml`: `on_hold: {max_days: 60}`; `policy.py`'s strict loader learns the section; `expire_bd`
   is read for the four states above.
7. **API.** `router.py`: `POST /api/engagements/{id}/request-info`, `/answer-info`, `/pause`, `/resume` (bodies in
   `schemas.py`); `EngagementDetail` gains `notes: list[NoteOut]` (kind, body, resume_at, by: party, at) in event
   order and `paused_from: EngagementState | None` (the state a side state will return to) so the stepper can keep
   the stage marked. `make openapi api-types`.
