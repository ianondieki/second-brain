# P27 — The working round: deadlines, the pipeline, team triage, prior-knowledge declarations, D3, offline trackers (the owner's brief, 2026-10-12)

The owner, after P26: "do another super round and improve adding more relevant features." Under the standing mandate
(keep iterating; gated rounds merge into the integration branch). This round builds six features that the product's
core loop was still missing, each one spec-backed or an owner-authorised addition in the P22 manner, both sides where
the loop has two sides, with no new vendor (D-36), no agent acting for anyone (principle 3), no personal data beyond
what the profile holds (principle 5), and nothing of an organisation's internal work visible to a developer.
Authority order: CLAUDE.md, docs/spec/04 (principles), docs/spec/07, REQ-UX-01..07, docs/spec/06 §6.4 (provenance),
§6.9 (tracker), §6.11 (reminders), docs/spec/03 (roles), docs/spec/10 (personal data), D-36, D-55/D-66/D-67, this card.

## The goal

By the end of P27: both sides see every deadline they carry on a month calendar and can subscribe to it from any
calendar app; an organisation reads its engagements as a pipeline (five stage columns, ageing, what has stalled, a
funnel), assigns each to a member and lets reviewers recommend internally without changing state (docs/spec/03's
`reviewer` role, unbuilt until now); an organisation can make the spec's prior-knowledge declaration inside its
10-business-day window, with only a hash leaving its machine, and the owner sees it; a developer can state their
GitHub, KECOBO and KIPI references as self-reported on their certificate and is nudged, once, before first sharing a
full proposal; and the trackers a person opened recently stay readable when the connection drops, with a data-saver
switch for thin connections.

## The contract (both sides build to this; the backend freezes `backend/openapi.json` before the screens start)

**A. Deadlines.**
- `GET /api/me/deadlines?from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD` (developer; default: overdue ones plus the next 90 days) and
  `GET /api/orgs/{org_id}/deadlines` (members): `{items: DeadlineOut[], now}` where `DeadlineOut = {key, kind:
  "stage"|"answer"|"milestone"|"brief"|"event", title, context (the other party or the Brief's/event's name), href
  (the portal path), due_at (UTC instant), due_on (Nairobi date), business_days_left, overdue, mine (the viewer's
  side acts), engagement_id|null}`; `key` is `"{kind}:{id}"`. Sorted by `due_at`. Ended and on-hold engagements
  contribute nothing; a hold's resume date is not a deadline.
- `GET /api/me/deadlines/{kind}/{id}/calendar.ics` and the org form: one VEVENT (RFC 5545, events/calendar.py's
  writer reused), DTSTART at `due_at`, 15 minutes long, SUMMARY "{title} — due", URL to the tracker.
- Feeds: `POST /api/me/calendar-feed` → `{url}` (creates or rotates; the token is 32 random bytes, base64url, stored
  only as its SHA-256; one live feed per user, and per (user, org) for an organisation member through
  `POST /api/orgs/{org_id}/calendar-feed`); `DELETE` revokes. `GET /api/calendar/{token}.ics` is unauthenticated
  (the token is the capability), answers 404 for an unknown or revoked token, never sets cookies, is rate limited
  (60/h per token), stamps `last_fetched_at`, and carries the same items as the deadlines list as VEVENTs with
  `X-WR-CALNAME`. A feed shows titles and dates only: never a note, a message or a proposal's text.

**B. Pipeline.** `GET /api/orgs/{org_id}/pipeline` → `{columns: [{group: "review"|"contact_nda"|"agreement"|
"implementation"|"close", count, median_days_in_stage|null, items: PipelineItem[]}], funnel: {window_days: 90,
entered: {review, contact_nda, agreement, implementation, close}, closed, ended}, stalled_after_bd, now}`.
`PipelineItem = EngagementSummary & {stage_entered_at, last_event_at, days_in_stage, stalled, team: TeamFacts|null}`.
`stalled` is true when the engagement is active, not on hold, and has had no engagement event for
`policy.pipeline.stalled_after_bd` (default 5) business days. Ended engagements sit in no column; CLOSED sits in
Close. The funnel counts engagements that entered each group inside the window (from `engagement_events`).

**C. Team triage.**
- `TeamFacts = {assignee: {user_id, display_name}|null, recommendations: {pursue, pass, discuss}, mine:
  {stance, note}|null}`; present on organisation-side reads only: `/api/orgs/{org_id}/engagements` items, the org
  tracker detail, the pipeline, the inbox items. **Never on a developer-side response**: the developer's
  `EngagementSummary`/`EngagementDetail` carry no `team` field at all (a test asserts the key is absent).
- `PUT /api/orgs/{org_id}/engagements/{id}/assignee` body `{user_id: UUID|null}`: owner, admin, reviewer or
  signatory may assign; the assignee must be a member with one of those roles; 409 when unchanged. One live row per
  engagement (`engagement_assignments`, previous row closed with `unassigned_at`). N30 to the assignee (in-app) unless
  self-assigned. An `audit_events` row (ids only).
- `PUT /api/orgs/{org_id}/engagements/{id}/recommendation` body `{stance: "pursue"|"pass"|"discuss", note: str ≤
  200}` (owner, admin, reviewer, signatory; one row per member per engagement, upserted); `DELETE` removes the
  caller's. `GET /api/orgs/{org_id}/engagements/{id}/team` → `{members: [{user_id, display_name, role}], assignee,
  recommendations: [{user_id, display_name, stance, note, updated_at}]}` for members only. The note is plain text,
  rendered as text, never in an email, a log or a developer-visible response; a member who leaves the organisation
  keeps nothing visible (rows stay for the organisation; RLS by org).

**D. Prior-knowledge declaration.**
- The window: 10 Kenyan business days (the holidays file) from the organisation's first `document_views` row for the
  engagement's proposal (`first_tier2_view_at`), closing at the end of that day in Nairobi (`window_closes_on`).
  The org tracker detail carries `prior_knowledge: {window_closes_on, first_tier2_view_at, declared_at|null,
  can_declare}`; the developer's detail carries `prior_knowledge: {org_name, declared_at, evidence_sha256_hex,
  evidence_title, evidence_dated_on}|null`.
- `POST /api/orgs/{org_id}/engagements/{id}/prior-knowledge` body `{evidence_sha256_hex: 64 hex, evidence_title:
  str ≤ 200, evidence_dated_on: date ≤ today}` by signatory, admin or owner → 201; 403 `no_tier2_access_yet` before
  any Tier-2 view; 403 `declaration_window_closed` with the spec's sentence after it; 409 `already_declared` (one
  per engagement). The row (`prior_knowledge_declarations`) is append-only (triggers as on audit tables), hashed into
  the engagement's event chain as an engagement event `prior_knowledge_declared` (payload: ids and the hash only),
  plus an `audit_events` row; N31 to the owner (in-app + status email naming the organisation and the idea's title,
  never the evidence title). The evidence title is the organisation's own words: plain text, ≤ 200, rendered as text.
- No file is ever uploaded for this: the browser hashes the file with Web Crypto and sends the hex.

**E. D3 and the nudge.** `developer_profiles` gains `github_login` (GitHub's rules, ≤ 39), `kecobo_number` and
`kipi_number` (≤ 40 printable characters each), all optional, on `ProfileOut` and `PATCH /api/me/profile`; the
certificate PDF prints them under one line "Self-reported references" when any is set (the label is
[[COPY-REVIEW]]); the public `/verify` page never shows them. `ip_nudge_dismissed_at` on the profile; the tracker's
developer detail carries `ip_nudge: bool` (true until dismissed, only when the developer's next step is `send_nda`
and the proposal has no Tier-2 view yet); `POST /api/me/profile/ip-nudge/dismiss`.

**F. Offline.** No API. A hand-written service worker at `/sw.js` (no library): `/_next/static/*` cache-first;
the HTML of `/dev/engagements`, `/org/engagements`, `/dev/engagements/{id}`, `/org/engagements/{id}` network-first
with the cached copy when the network fails; a small `/offline` page for anything else. The cache is named by the
signed-in user's hash (the shell tells the worker on load; a different hash deletes the other users' caches), is
deleted on sign-out, and holds at most 20 trackers for 7 days. **Never cached**: `/api/*`, the messages pages, any
full-proposal or Tier-2 render route, the inbox, anything under `/org/inbox`, `/billing`, `/settings`, `/admin`.
Responses carry `Cache-Control: private, no-store` as today; the worker's cache is the only copy. The shell shows an
"offline, as of <time EAT>" banner from the cached response's `Date`. Data saver: a `data-saver=1` cookie (one year,
`SameSite=Lax`, not `HttpOnly` so the switch can read it) that the server treats exactly like `Save-Data: on`.

## Acceptance (measurable; the tests are the round's)

1. **A.** A developer with a SUBMITTED engagement due 16 Oct, an INFO_REQUESTED one (answer by 20 Oct) and a Brief
   they pitched to closing 30 Oct sees three entries on Engagements › Calendar in October, the first with the live
   clock; the organisation's calendar shows its stage deadlines, its Briefs' deadlines and its events; each entry's
   `.ics` opens with the right instant in Nairobi; a feed URL created in Settings returns the same entries to an
   unauthenticated `GET`, a rotated URL makes the old one 404, a revoked one 404; the feed never contains a note,
   message or proposal text (test asserts on the seeded data). Unit: business days and the Nairobi day boundary.
2. **B.** Telco A's board shows its seeded engagements in the right columns with counts; an engagement with no
   event for 6 business days on the test clock is marked stalled; the funnel's `entered` counts match the events in
   the window; the board is server-rendered, scrolls sideways on a phone with no page overflow, keeps ≤ 2 chips per
   card, and the list, calendar and board are one `TabNav`.
3. **C.** A reviewer at Telco A assigns an engagement to a colleague who gets N30 and sees it under "Assigned to
   me"; a reviewer's recommendation shows to another member with its note and in the header's tally; the developer's
   tracker, list and `GET /api/me/engagements/{id}` carry no `team` key and no recommendation text (asserted); a
   `viewer` or `finance` member cannot assign or recommend (403); a member of another organisation gets 404.
4. **D.** After Telco A's first Tier-2 view on the test clock, its signatory declares a hash within 10 business days
   (201); the developer gets N31 and sees the declaration (org name, hash, title, date) on the tracker; day 11 refuses
   with `declaration_window_closed` and the spec's sentence, and the action is gone from the screen; before any view
   it refuses `no_tier2_access_yet`; a second declaration refuses 409; the row cannot be updated or deleted (trigger
   test); the event chain verifies; the browser-side hash of a fixture file equals `sha256sum`.
5. **E.** A developer saves `github_login`, `kecobo_number`, `kipi_number`; an invalid login is refused 422; the
   certificate PDF text contains the self-reported line and `/verify/{cert}` does not; the nudge shows once with the
   `send_nda` action and not after dismissal or once a Tier-2 view exists; copy tagged [[COPY-REVIEW]].
6. **F.** Playwright: a developer opens a tracker, goes offline (`context.setOffline(true)`), reloads: the tracker
   renders from the worker's cache with the "as of" banner and no action is clickable (the page is read-only
   offline: every form control disabled by the banner's script); an unvisited tracker shows `/offline`; the messages
   page and the inbox are never served from cache (offline → `/offline`); sign-out deletes the cache (a reload
   offline shows `/offline`); a second user's sign-in on the same browser deletes the first user's cache; axe strict 0
   on the banner and `/offline`. The data-saver switch sets the cookie and the next page carries no photograph
   (`[data-niche-band="lattice"]` everywhere, the greeting without its photograph).
7. **Every track.** reviewer PASS per worktree; security-reviewer PASS for C, D, F (and the calendar feed of A);
   ux-reviewer PASS on the screens; vitest, eslint, tsc, prettier (width 120 on touched files), copy-lint, backend
   ruff/format/mypy --strict/pytest; every route ≤ 150,000 B gzipped JS (the board and calendar are server-rendered;
   the worker is not route JS); axe strict 0 at 360/1440 light and dark on the calendar, the board, the team sheet,
   the declaration sheet, `/offline`; Lighthouse mobile performance ≥ 90 and accessibility ≥ 95 on the calendar and
   the board; the full Playwright suite, the clock scenarios and the demo story green on the compose stack; CI as
   the standing exceptions; `check_traceability.py` PASS; screenshots `p27` at 375/1440 light and dark.
