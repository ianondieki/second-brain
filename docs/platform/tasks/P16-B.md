**Deviations and findings**

- Loading states have costs on direct loads. With `loading.tsx` a page streams: (a) a signed-out visit to a signed-in
  URL now answers 200 with the skeleton and a streamed redirect (`<meta http-equiv="refresh" content="1;url=/login">`
  plus the client redirect) instead of a 307; (b) React 19.2 reveals streamed content no sooner than ~300 ms after the
  fallback painted, so a fast page shows the skeleton for about 0.3 s on a full load (measured: load at 129 ms,
  content revealed at ~300 ms). Client navigations get the instant prefetched frame, which is the point. A cheap
  proxy redirect for requests with no session cookie would restore the 307 (proxy.ts is auth code, not in this
  card: needs a decision and the security reviewer).
- The e2e helpers now wait for a streamed page to settle (`settled()` in `e2e/support/screen.ts`: no
  `[data-skeleton]` and a non-empty title) inside `checkScreen` and `expectSeparateTargets`, and once in the scout
  walk before a strict `locator("main")` after a direct load. Same checks, run on the page the person sees.
- Loading states must hold no client component and no `next/link`: Next prefetches them for every link in view, and
  their references pulled 3.6–12 KB of route chunks (Turbopack merges `next/link` into a route's own chunk). Hence
  link-free bases (`ShellFrame`, `TopBarBase`, `PortalNavBase`, `nav-sections.ts`, `anchor.ts`), plain `<a>` links
  and a still avatar in the loading frame. Loading states cannot read `?org=`, so an org member of several
  organisations sees plain section links without it until the page arrives.
- Not-found: group `not-found.tsx` files only render for `notFound()` calls, never for unknown addresses, so a root
  `app/not-found.tsx` does that job (same component). It reads the session to pick the frame; the staff console still
  answers non-staff exactly like an unknown address (research e2e green).
- Row follows the plan (hairline above every row, none under the list); `dev/page.tsx` still wraps EngagementRow in its
  own `ul.border-b` until part C. `RowList` wraps each Row in its own `li`; Row is the `article`.
- Callout tones are `info|ok|error|neutral` (Alert's names plus neutral), not Badge's `accent`.
- Tracker Chip keeps its six tones (On hold in ink, which Badge's four do not have) on the Badge's shape.
- Danger is outlined everywhere (DeleteIdea's confirm button was filled; WithdrawTag's confirm was primary-styled).
  Its hover mixes into `--field` (`--error-wash`), not `--paper` as the old outlines did: a barely visible difference.
- The two `jacaranda 45% paper` mixes (idea editor stepper, checkout) match no token and stay inline (part C).
- Portal tab bar at 360 px: "Engagements" in bold overlapped "Companies" (also on the integration build); tabs now
  share the bar by label width (`flex-auto`).
- The loading skeleton's hidden sentence is an `sr-only` h1 (axe `page-has-heading-one`).
- One commit (9d063ed) also carries the first version of the e2e `settled()` helper.
