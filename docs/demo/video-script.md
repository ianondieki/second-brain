# Wazo in sixty seconds (video script)

Cut from the walkthrough recording (`make demo-walkthrough`, 1280 × 720, `docs/demo/video/`) or re-shot by hand on the
seeded demo. One voice, plain words, no claims about protection or figures; every screen is the real prototype with
demo data. Timings are approximate; the recording's pauses are 1.2 s per beat.

| Time | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | The landing page: the lattice band, the headline, the product visual settling | A developer with an idea. An organisation with a problem. Wazo is where the two meet, on the record. |
| 0:06–0:12 | Amina's first login: the three-step tour, Next, Next, Done | Amina signs in. A short tour, three cards, and she is home. |
| 0:12–0:20 | Home: the four tiles, "Needs you" with the deadline and "Open the tracker", the recommended problems | Home shows what needs her now, the deadline, and problems chosen for her, with the reasons. |
| 0:20–0:27 | Discover: trending problems as cards; one opens "More about this problem" with its sources; the Briefs tab, one Brief with its budget band and deadline | Discover shows what is moving this week, with the sources behind each problem, and the Briefs organisations post when they want proposals. |
| 0:27–0:35 | The idea's certificate sheet with the seal; cut to `/verify`, signed out, the fingerprint checked | Every published idea gets a certificate: a fingerprint and an independent timestamp. Anyone can check it, nobody sees the idea. |
| 0:35–0:43 | Telco A's reviewer: the Inbox, the Evaluation NDA, the full proposal marked with her name | An organisation reads the full proposal only after the NDA, and every copy carries the reader's name. |
| 0:43–0:51 | The tracker: whose turn, the five stages; Telco A's question waiting for Brian, his bell, the answer; the stage moves | Both sides follow one tracker: whose turn it is, what is due, a question and its answer, and the agreement, step by step. |
| 0:51–0:57 | Plan & billing, the simulated M-Pesa handset confirming; Mailpit's branded daily nudge | Paying works the way Kenya pays. And the day's reminders arrive by email. |
| 0:57–1:00 | The landing page again, the wordmark and the seal | Wazo. Ideas, on the record. |
